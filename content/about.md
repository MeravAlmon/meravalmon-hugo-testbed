For over 15 years, I have worked with people seeking a personal approach to their health. What matters to me is taking the time to understand your experience and making room for your questions.

## How I work

My practice centers on palpation-based Japanese acupuncture. I use careful observation and touch to guide treatment, then reassess your response as the session progresses. Your comfort and feedback are part of that process.

Rather than use the same combination of techniques for everyone, I choose an approach around your history, your current concerns, and what we find during the assessment.

## Training and experience

My mentor, **David Euler**, has been an important influence on my training in palpation-based Japanese acupuncture.

My additional studies include:

- **Auriculotherapy** with Dr. Nadia Volf.
- **Scalp acupuncture** with Darrell Wallace.
- **Microneedling techniques** with Josh Nerenberg of San Diego Cosmetic Acupuncture.

I have also been part of the teaching staff for a palpation-based acupuncture program for practitioners at Harvard Medical School.

## Care in a shared clinic setting

I see patients at Newton Acupuncture Clinic, at 1647 Washington Street in West Newton. My practice includes acupuncture, selected complementary techniques, and cosmetic skin treatments.

If you are unsure whether to start with acupuncture or a cosmetic appointment, you are welcome to ask. I am happy to explain what each visit involves before you decide.
