You do not need to know anything about acupuncture before coming in. Your first appointment is a chance for us to talk about your concerns, review your health history, and discuss a plan you feel comfortable with.

## During your first appointment

I begin with a conversation and an assessment, including gentle examination through touch. I explain what I recommend and invite your questions before we proceed. We may also discuss practical changes to your daily routine where relevant.

Let me know if you are nervous about needles, have had a difficult treatment experience, or need a different position to feel comfortable.

## Before you arrive

- Wear loose, comfortable clothing.
- Bring information about your medications, supplements, and relevant medical care.
- Complete the medical history form and bring it with you. If you cannot print it, contact me before your visit so we can arrange how to complete it.
- Mention any mobility, sensory, or other access needs when scheduling.

## Questions people often ask

### Does acupuncture hurt?

I use fine needles and a gentle approach, but sensations differ from person to person. You may feel a brief prick or pressure. Let me know if you are uncomfortable so I can adjust the treatment.

### How many visits will I need?

That depends on your concerns and how you respond. We will discuss an initial plan and review it together, rather than promise a particular result or number of sessions.

### Can I keep seeing my other health-care providers?

Yes. Acupuncture can be part of a broader care plan. Keep your providers informed and continue prescribed treatment unless your prescribing clinician advises otherwise.
