At your initial appointment, we will begin with a comprehensive evaluation and examination. Following this, we'll go over the proposed treatment plan and discuss any recommended dietary adjustments. Please feel free to ask any questions to ensure you're fully comfortable and informed about the next steps in your care.

For your comfort, we recommend wearing loose, comfortable clothing.

## Medical History Form
