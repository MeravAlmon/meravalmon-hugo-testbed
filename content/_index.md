Merav Almon-Feigin is a dedicated and skilled Japanese acupuncturist based in Newton, Massachusetts. With a passion for holistic healing and a deep respect for traditional practices, Merav combines her extensive training in Japanese acupuncture with a compassionate approach to patient care.

I trained with David Euler in palpation based acupuncture (Japanese acupuncture), Dr. Nadia Volf in auriculotherapy, Darrell Wallace in scalp acupuncture, and Josh Nerenberg of San Diego Cosmetic Acupuncture, in microneedling techniques. Additionally, I have been part of the teaching staff at the palpation-based acupuncture program for practitioners at Harvard Medical School. This extensive training enhances my expertise and understanding of various acupuncture modalities.

I believe in the body's inherent ability to heal itself and work closely with my clients to support their journey toward wellness. By utilizing gentle techniques and a focus on individualized treatment plans, I aim to address a wide range of health concerns, from chronic pain and stress to digestive issues and overall wellness.

My commitment to fostering a nurturing environment allows patients to feel comfortable and supported during their sessions. I am not only dedicated to my practice, but also actively engage with the community, sharing the benefits of acupuncture and promoting holistic health.

## The Clinic

Nestled in the heart of Newton, Massachusetts, I offer a unique and holistic approach to wellness through Japanese acupuncture. With over 15 years of experience, I specialize in palpation-based acupuncture, which is the primary method I use in my practice.

In addition to acupuncture, I incorporate cold laser therapy and kinesiology tape into my treatments, enhancing the healing process and addressing a diverse range of health concerns. My training also includes specialized modalities such as auriculotherapy, scalp acupuncture, and microneedling, ensuring that each treatment is tailored to the individual needs of my clients.
