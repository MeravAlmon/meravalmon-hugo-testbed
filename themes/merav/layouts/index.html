MEET MERAV

Meet Merav
Almon-Feigin.

I practice Japanese acupuncture with an emphasis on gentle technique, careful assessment, and a conversation you can take part in.

My mentor, David Euler, has shaped my training in palpation-based acupuncture. I also draw on training in auriculotherapy, scalp acupuncture, and microneedling, and have been part of the teaching staff for a palpation-based acupuncture program for practitioners at Harvard Medical School.

I’ll explain my recommendations, make room for your questions, and check in about your comfort throughout treatment.
