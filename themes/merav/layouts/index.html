MEET MERAV

Rooted in experience.
Guided by you.

I’m Merav Almon-Feigin, a Japanese acupuncturist with over 15 years of experience and a deep respect for the person behind every treatment.

I specialize in palpation-based acupuncture, combining gentle techniques with an individualized approach. My practice also includes cold laser therapy, kinesiology taping, auriculotherapy, scalp acupuncture, and microneedling.

My training includes study with David Euler, Dr. Nadia Volf, Darrell Wallace, and Josh Nerenberg. I have also been part of the teaching staff in the palpation-based acupuncture program for practitioners at Harvard Medical School.
